## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Copy the block below into the "Full description" field in Play Console.

```
Transform your upper body strength with Keep Pushing, the 30-day push-up challenge app designed to take you from your current level to your goal.

WHY KEEP PUSHING?
• Personalized 30-day plan – Set your starting point and target
• Daily workouts – Progressive training from day 1 to day 30
• Track progress – Mark completed days and watch your streak grow
• Smart reminders – Never miss a workout with customizable notifications
• Cross-device sync – Sign in with Google to sync across devices
• Share achievements – Post your progress to LinkedIn

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set your goal – Choose your current ability and target (e.g. 5 to 50 push-ups)
2. Follow your plan – Get a structured 30-day training program
3. Complete daily workouts – Each day builds on the previous
4. Track your progress – See your streak and completion percentage
5. Reach your goal – Hit your target by day 30

FEATURES
• Progressive training that scales to your level
• Daily reminder notifications at your chosen time
• Dashboard with streak, progress %, and days remaining
• One-tap to mark days complete
• Dark theme
• Works offline – data stored on your device
• Optional Google sign-in for multi-device sync

WHO IS THIS FOR?
Whether you're a beginner or want to improve your push-up count, Keep Pushing adapts to your level. No equipment needed – train anywhere, anytime.

PRIVACY
Your data is stored on your device. Optional sign-in syncs only to your account. We don't sell your data. See our privacy policy for details.

Start your 30-day journey today. Download Keep Pushing.
```

## Privacy policy URL – where to host

You need a public URL for your privacy policy. Options:

1. **GitHub Pages** – Create a repo, enable Pages, put legal/privacy-policy.html in the repo and use:  
   https://yourusername.github.io/your-repo/legal/privacy-policy.html

2. **Same domain as your app** – If you have a site:  
   https://yourdomain.com/privacy-policy.html

3. **Vercel/Netlify** – Deploy the legal folder and use the URL they give you.

Replace support@keeppushing.app in the HTML files with your real email before going live.
