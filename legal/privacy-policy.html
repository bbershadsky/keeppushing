Privacy Policy for Keep Pushing

Last updated: February 2025

Keep Pushing ("we", "our", or "the app") is a 30-day push-up challenge tracker. This policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Data stored only on your device (no account)

Challenge progress: Your start date, target, completed days, and workout preferences are stored locally on your device (localStorage). This data is not sent to our servers unless you sign in.

1.2 Data we process when you sign in with Google

If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, display name, and email address from Google. We use this to associate your challenge data with your account and to sync your progress across devices. Your challenge data is stored in our database (Neon PostgreSQL) so you can access it from other devices.

1.3 Push notifications

If you enable daily reminders, on Android we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Your device receives a unique token that we may store on our servers to send you reminder notifications at the time you choose. We do not use push notifications for marketing; we only send the reminders you configure.

1.4 Usage we do not collect

We do not collect location data, track your activity across other apps or websites, sell your data to third parties, or use your data for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run the app (show your challenge, track completed days, sync across devices if you sign in), to send reminders if you enable them, and to improve the service using anonymized, aggregated data (e.g. crash reports). We do not use this to identify you.

3. Data Sharing

We use Google (Sign-In), Firebase (Cloud Messaging), and Neon (database) as described. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing.

4. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. For signed-in users, we retain account and challenge data until you request deletion. We retain push notification tokens only as long as you have notifications enabled.

5. Security

We use HTTPS for all network communication. Passwords are not stored by us; sign-in is handled by Google. We take reasonable measures to protect data stored on our servers.

6. Your Rights

You can access and correct your data in the app. To request deletion of your account and data from our servers, contact us at the email below. You can disable reminders at any time.

7. Children

Keep Pushing is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, deletion requests, or complaints: support@keeppushing.app (replace with your actual support email). If you are in the EEA, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
