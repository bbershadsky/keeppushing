Delete your account and data – Keep Pushing

Keep Pushing is the 30-day push-up challenge app. If you want your account and associated data removed from our servers, follow the steps below.

Steps to request account and data deletion

  1. Send an email to b.boriz@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Keep Pushing account so we can process it quickly.
  3. Include the email address you used to sign in to Keep Pushing (Google account email), so we can find your account.
  4. We will confirm once your account and data have been deleted, usually within 30 days of your request.

What we delete

When you request account deletion, we delete the following from our systems:

  • Your account (Google sign-in link and profile info)
  • Your challenge data (start date, completed days, preferences) stored on our servers
  • Any push notification token (FCM) we have stored for your account

What we do not delete (and why)

  • Data on your device: Progress and preferences stored locally on your phone are not under our control. To remove them, clear the app’s data in your device settings or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: We may retain your data in backups for a short period (e.g. up to 90 days) before they are overwritten; we do not use this data for any other purpose.

No account? (used the app without signing in)

If you never signed in with Google, we do not have your account or challenge data on our servers. All your data is only on your device. To remove it, clear the app data or uninstall Keep Pushing.

For other privacy questions, see our Privacy policy.
